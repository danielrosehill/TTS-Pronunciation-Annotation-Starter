Screen, then rank

Pass 1, Screen: judge the whole clip, the sentence as rendered: Pass (P) or Fail (X), and the voice gender (F M U). A fail needs one tap on a reason category (1–6 while failing); pick all that apply. Add detail is optional: word-level issues and quality ratings, on a pass or a fail, never required.

Pass 2, Rank: two passing clips of the same sentence, blind. A / B play them; 1 A better, 2 B better, 3 tie, 4 both bad (both become fails); Enter confirms, U undoes the last one.

Word tags

Answer each row for the clip: scales are 1 = poor to 5 = native-perfect, yes/no rows are a plain yes or no, and choice rows pick one option. Leave a row empty if you can't judge it. Then mark word-level problems: tap the word.

  • Stress: pick the syllable the voice stressed and the one it should have. The reference syllables and stress are preselected when the evaluation provides them.
  • Mispronounced: write what you heard and what was expected in IPA (the keypad has the common symbols). Annotators are expected to know IPA.
  • Other: skipped, added sound, unnatural pause, accent… with a comment. Shift-tap to tag a span.
